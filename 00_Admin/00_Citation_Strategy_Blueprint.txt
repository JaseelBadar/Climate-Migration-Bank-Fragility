WHAT KILLS CITATIONS (Avoid These)
================================================================================

❌ PUBLISHING IN LOW-VISIBILITY JOURNAL
   - Better to wait 6 months for top journal than rush to Tier-3
   - Tier-3 journals get 1/10th the readership

❌ HIDING BEHIND PAYWALL WITHOUT WORKING PAPER
   - Always have free SSRN version even after publication
   - 60% of citations come from working paper version, not final publication

❌ COMPLICATED WRITING
   - If only 20 people understand your paper, max 20 citations
   - Clear writing = broader audience = more citations

❌ NO REPLICATION MATERIALS
   - Reviewers remember papers they can verify
   - If they can't replicate, they don't trust → don't cite

❌ OVERSTATED CLAIMS
   - If you claim "proof" but paper shows "correlation," reviewers dismiss
   - Conservative claims = credible = cited more

❌ IGNORING TWITTER/SOCIAL MEDIA
   - 2026 reality: Papers with social media presence get 30% more citations
   - Not because Twitter matters, but because it signals visibility
