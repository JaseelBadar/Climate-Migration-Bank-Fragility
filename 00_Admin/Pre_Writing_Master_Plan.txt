SECTION 7: CRITICAL METHODOLOGICAL CONSTRAINTS FOR WRITING
----------------------------------------------------------------------

These must be enforced throughout every draft. No exceptions.

1. H2 null reconciliation: Deposit effects are lagged (H3), not
   contemporaneous. IV tests zero-effect window by construction.
   Never present H2 null as a contradiction of the mechanism.

2. H4c language constraint: EXACT required text --
   "Monsoon seasonality moderates the deposit response to moderate-
   intensity floods (Rule A: beta = +0.012, p < 0.001) but not to
   severe flood events (Rule B: p = 0.774). The heterogeneity result
   is fragile to flood intensity definition."

3. Rule B IV (H2): Must be labeled suggestive throughout.
   F = 8.949 < 10. Causal language removed from abstract and
   conclusions for Rule B IV results.

4. H4a: Must document that Feb 6 result (p < 0.001) was spurious --
   result of homonymous FE collapse. Correct result is null.
   The methodological correction must be stated explicitly.

5. Proxy discipline: urban_proxy and high_exposure_proxy are proxies.
   Never claim census-based urban/rural heterogeneity.

6. TUENSANG disclosure: Must flag the within-year reversal (+3.21)
   as a likely reporting artifact in the Appendix or footnote.

7. Demonetization gap: 2016Q3-2017Q1 absent from panel. Must be
   named explicitly in the Data section.

8. Nominal INR: Deposits in nominal Rupees. Quarter FE absorb national
   price trends. Acknowledged as a limitation.

9. linearmodels SE: Current paper tables use statsmodels (conservative
   SEs). Final paper tables must use linearmodels PanelOLS results
   (Scripts 27b-30b). Never mix the two in the same table.

10. Northeast: If Script 33 shows H3 t-2 disappears after NE exclusion,
    writing must be reframed immediately. Do not begin Results writing
    until Script 33 output is verified.
