These are enforced throughout every draft. No exceptions. No softening.

1.  H2 null reconciliation:
    Deposit effects are lagged (H3), not contemporaneous. The IV window
    by construction tests contemporaneous transmission. H2 null is
    expected and consistent with H3 confirmed. Never present H2 null
    as a contradiction of the mechanism. Never use the phrase
    "H2 fails" -- it does not fail; it finds exactly what the mechanism
    predicts for the contemporaneous window.

2.  H4c language constraint. EXACT REQUIRED TEXT (no paraphrase):
    "Monsoon seasonality moderates the deposit response to moderate-
    intensity floods (Rule A: beta = +0.012, p < 0.001) but not to
    severe flood events (Rule B: p = 0.774). The heterogeneity result
    is fragile to flood intensity definition."

3.  Rule B IV (H2): labeled suggestive throughout.
    F = 8.949 < 10. Causal language removed from abstract and
    conclusions for Rule B IV results. Report F-stat in the table.

4.  H4a spurious benchmark disclosure:
    Must state explicitly that the Feb 6 H4a result (p < 0.001) was
    spurious -- caused by homonymous FE collapse (district_gadm alone,
    624 FE instead of 631). Corrected result is null. The methodological
    correction must be documented in the paper (footnote or Appendix).

5.  Proxy discipline:
    urban_proxy and high_exposure_proxy are proxies. Never claim
    census-based urban/rural heterogeneity. Every mention of these
    variables must carry the word "proxy."

6.  TUENSANG disclosure:
    Flag the within-year reversal (+3.21) as a likely reporting artifact.
    Location: Appendix or footnote in the Data section.

7.  Demonetization gap:
    2016Q3-2017Q1 is absent from panel (RBI blackout).

11. R2 pre-trend disclosure. MANDATORY -- no exceptions.
    Test 2 Rule A: beta = +0.004664, p = 0.011 (no district FE).
    Test 2b Rule A: beta = +0.004417, p = 0.019 (with district FE).
    Direction POSITIVE -- opposite sign to H3 t-2 (-0.007005).
    This is the two-phase household liquidity cycle:
      Phase 1 (t-1 pre-period): anticipatory accumulation (+0.005)
      Phase 2 (t-2 post-flood): deposit withdrawal              (-0.007)
    This must be disclosed in full in Section 6 (Robustness).
    REQUIRED LANGUAGE (exact):
    "Flood exposure at time t predicts higher deposit growth at t-1
    (beta = +0.005, p = 0.011), a signal that survives the inclusion
    of district fixed effects (beta = +0.004, p = 0.019). This
    pre-period effect is positive and opposite in sign to the t-2
    withdrawal effect (-0.007), indicating a two-phase household
    liquidity cycle -- anticipatory saving ahead of the predictable
    flood season followed by post-flood deposit withdrawal -- rather
    than a pre-existing downward trend in deposits."
    Never claim the R2 placebo is clean. Never omit the pre-period.
    Never describe the two-phase cycle as a weakness. It is a finding.
