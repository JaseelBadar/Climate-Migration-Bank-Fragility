SECTION 7: CRITICAL METHODOLOGICAL CONSTRAINTS FOR WRITING
----------------------------------------------------------------------

These must be enforced throughout every draft. No exceptions.

1. H2 null reconciliation: Deposit effects are lagged (H3), not
   contemporaneous. IV tests zero-effect window by construction.
   Never present H2 null as a contradiction of the mechanism.

2. H4c language constraint: EXACT required text --
   "Monsoon seasonality moderates the deposit response to moderate-
   intensity floods (Rule A: beta = +0.012, p < 0.001) but not to
   severe flood events (Rule B: p = 0.774). The heterogeneity result
   is fragile to flood intensity definition."

3. Rule B IV (H2): Must be labeled suggestive throughout.
   F = 8.949 < 10. Causal language removed from abstract and
   conclusions for Rule B IV results.

4. H4a: Must document that Feb 6 result (p < 0.001) was spurious --
   result of homonymous FE collapse. Correct result is null.
   The methodological correction must be stated explicitly.

5. Proxy discipline: urban_proxy and high_exposure_proxy are proxies.
   Never claim census-based urban/rural heterogeneity.

6. TUENSANG disclosure: Must flag the within-year reversal (+3.21)
   as a likely reporting artifact in the Appendix or footnote.

7. Demonetization gap: 2016Q3-2017Q1 absent from panel. Must be
   named explicitly in the Data section.

8. Nominal INR: Deposits in nominal Rupees. Quarter FE absorb national
   price trends. Acknowledged as a limitation.

9. linearmodels SE: Final paper tables must use linearmodels PanelOLS
   results (Scripts 27b-30b). Never mix statsmodels and linearmodels
   results in the same table.

10. Northeast: Script 33 confirms H3 t-2 robust to NE exclusion.
    H3 result is not NE-driven. Writing is unblocked on this dimension.
    NE exclusion result reported in robustness section.

11. R2 pre-trend disclosure (MANDATORY -- no exceptions):
    Test 2 Rule A: beta = +0.004664, p = 0.011 (no district FE).
    Test 2b Rule A: beta = +0.004417, p = 0.019 (with district FE).
    Direction is POSITIVE -- opposite sign to H3 t-2 (-0.007005).
    This is NOT a spurious trend. It reflects anticipatory saving by
    households in flood-prone districts ahead of the predictable
    monsoon flood season.
    H3 identifies a two-phase liquidity cycle:
      Phase 1 (t-1): anticipatory accumulation (+0.005)
      Phase 2 (t-2): post-flood withdrawal (-0.007)
    This must be disclosed in full in the Robustness section.
    REQUIRED LANGUAGE (exact):
    "Flood exposure at time t predicts higher deposit growth at t-1
    (beta = +0.005, p = 0.011), a signal that survives the inclusion
    of district fixed effects (beta = +0.004, p = 0.019). This
    pre-period effect is positive and opposite in sign to the t-2
    withdrawal effect (-0.007), indicating a two-phase household
    liquidity cycle -- anticipatory saving ahead of the predictable
    flood season followed by post-flood deposit withdrawal -- rather
    than a pre-existing downward trend in deposits."
    Never claim R2 placebo is clean. Never omit the positive pre-period.
    Never describe the two-phase cycle as a weakness. It is a finding.

12. H4b winsorization failure (MANDATORY -- no exceptions):
    H4b Rule A: p = 0.020 (main, Script 30).
    H4b Rule A: p = 0.865 (winsorized, Script 37).
    H4b does not survive winsorization of 2.01% of observations.
    H4b is DEMOTED from "supported" to "suggestive."
    The net effect calculation (+0.0047 baseline, -0.0021 net) is
    a descriptive estimate from the non-winsorized baseline only.
    It cannot be presented as causal or robust.
    REQUIRED LANGUAGE (exact):
    "The high flood exposure interaction (H4b) is significant in the
    baseline specification (Rule A: beta = -0.007, p = 0.020) but
    does not survive winsorization of the top and bottom 1% of deposit
    growth observations (p = 0.865). The effect is sensitive to extreme
    observations and should be interpreted as suggestive evidence only."
    Never present H4b as a robust finding. Never omit the
    winsorization failure.

13. H1 state clustering (MANDATORY until Script 36b is complete):

    DO NOT write H1 robustness claims until Script 36b is complete.
